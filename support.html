Help with the things you might need most.

Forgot your caregiver PIN?

Use the PIN recovery instructions, then return to TalkBoard to set a new PIN. Your words and settings are kept.

Restore a purchase

In Caregiver Settings, open Unlock & Restore, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Having trouble hearing speech?

Check your device volume and the voice selected in TalkBoard’s voice settings. Some system voices may need to be downloaded in device settings for offline use.

Protect your words and settings

Keep an external backup in a location you choose. If your saved library cannot be opened, keep any existing backups and contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app.
