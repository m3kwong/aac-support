TalkBoard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

On-device Information

TalkBoard stores boards, personal words, selected photos, settings, the caregiver PIN representation, and the keyboard draft on this device. Speech uses the system speech service. TalkBoard has no account, advertising service, or app-operated cloud synchronization.

Your system backup service may also back up app data, depending on device settings. Named versions are local recovery copies, not a cloud backup service.

Device Activity

When device activity collection is enabled, TalkBoard saves word selections, board visits, speech requests, the text of spoken sentences and keyboard speech, timestamps, and session information locally. Reports summarize this activity and exclude raw typed sentences; they are not assessments of language development.

Activity stays on this device. Earlier exported backups and local versions may contain their own copies. Old activity is pruned when a new session begins, using the saved retention period.

Sharing and Purchases

Reports, printable boards, and backups are shared only through an export you choose. A backup can contain personal words, photos, the PIN representation, and activity history. Choose recipients carefully. Shared copies remain with the recipients even if you delete local data.

Purchases are handled by Apple. TalkBoard reads verified StoreKit entitlements to unlock these features and does not receive your payment-card details.

Feedback

Send Feedback opens an English Google Form in a browser. If you submit feedback, your message is sent to Google Forms and made available to the app developer. TalkBoard does not automatically attach your boards, photos, PIN, or communication activity. Avoid including sensitive personal information in your message.

The feedback form requires an internet connection. TalkBoard does not store an offline feedback draft or send feedback in the background.

Privacy Questions

Use Send Feedback in Settings for privacy questions or requests concerning feedback you previously submitted. Include enough information to identify the submission, without including your PIN or communication history.

Photos and Deletion

The photo picker gives TalkBoard the photos you select. TalkBoard does not upload them. Remove individual photos in the tile editor. Clear typed text from the keyboard screen. Manage or delete saved word boards and recovery copies in Saved Word Boards after unlocking. Removing the app removes its local data; exported copies and system backups may remain.
